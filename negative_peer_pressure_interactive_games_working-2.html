📚 What is Negative Peer Pressure?

Negative peer pressure happens when people feel pushed by friends or a group to do something harmful, uncomfortable, or against their values. You have the right to make your own choices and treat others with respect.

🌟 Your Message

Being inclusive means making others feel respected, welcomed and safe. If peer pressure makes you uncomfortable, you can say no, walk away, support a friend, and talk to a trusted adult.
